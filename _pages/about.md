Hi there! Great of you to stop by this website which is dedicated to knowing a little bit more about me and what I focus on in research. I am a master's student at the [Georgia Institute of Technology School of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/). I am grateful to be advised by Prof. [Xiao Liu](https://sites.google.com/site/liuxiaosite1/team?authuser=0) working on developing learning-enabled optimization methods for power systems. 

In Spring 2026, I graduated from GT with my B.S. in Industrial Engineering where I will continue with my education at GT for my master's degree in Operations Research starting Fall 2026 with a projected graduation of Spring 2027. My background leverages machine learning, optimization, and stochastic processes towards developing modern, scalable solutions which prioritizes reliability and equitability at its forefront. More than anything else, I am interested in synergies across communities, believing that cross-collaboration is vital for effective solutions by branching various schools of thought within and across communities and industries.
